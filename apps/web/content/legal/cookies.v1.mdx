# Cookie and Storage Inventory

| Name                  | Kind                           | Purpose                                        | Lifespan         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `__Secure-mb-session` | cookie, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax | signed-in session id                           | 30 days, rolling |
| `mb-anon`             | cookie, HttpOnly               | anonymous-viewer correlation id for telemetry  | 30 days          |
| `mb-consent`          | cookie                         | your analytics-consent choice (banner regions) | 13 months        |
| `mb-theme`            | localStorage                   | theme preference                               | until cleared    |
| `mb-compose-draft`    | sessionStorage                 | unsent compose buffer                          | tab lifetime     |

Nothing else. No advertising cookies, no cross-site identifiers, no
fingerprint storage, no third-party pixels. `Do Not Track` and `Sec-GPC`
disable all behavioural writes unconditionally (§13.9.1).
