# DMCA Notice and Takedown

Designated agent for copyright notifications: copyright@musebook.dev.

A compliant notice includes: identification of the work, identification of the
infringing material (post slug or media URL), contact information, a
good-faith statement, a statement of accuracy under penalty of perjury, and a
physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notices go to the same address with the §512(g) elements.

On a compliant notice the post is unpublished (`status = 'removed'`) while the
determination is pending, and the creator is notified. Repeat infringers lose
their accounts.
