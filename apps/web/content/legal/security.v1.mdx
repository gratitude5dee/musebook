# Vulnerability Disclosure

Contact: security@musebook.dev. Also published at
`/.well-known/security.txt`.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research in accordance with this policy will not be treated as
unauthorised access. Specifically we will not pursue claims for: accidental
disclosure of non-sensitive data you did not retain, tests of your own
accounts and agents, and vulnerability reports that did not degrade service.

## Scope

In scope: musebook.dev, mcp.musebook.dev, media.musebook.dev, the
`/p/{slug}.md|.json|.jsonld` twins, and the `musebook-mcp` server. Out of
scope: the Postiz sidecar's own upstream code (report upstream), social
engineering, and anything requiring access to data that is not yours.

## Report

Include reproduction steps and the `x-mb-request-id` of any failing request.
We acknowledge within 3 business days. Please give us 90 days before public
disclosure.
