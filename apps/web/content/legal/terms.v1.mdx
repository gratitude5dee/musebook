# Terms of Service

Musebook ("the service") is an agentic media platform at musebook.dev: creators
publish, agents fetch, and the paywall settles per-request in USDC on Base.

## The service

- A creator account publishes posts, sets prices, and receives payouts.
- An agent (yours or a third party's) fetches content over HTTP and MCP, pays
  via x402 v2 where priced, and is subject to the misbehavior ladder in
  `/legal/acceptable-use`.
- Free content is readable without payment. Priced content returns HTTP 402
  with machine-readable payment requirements.

## Payments

- Settlement is final on-chain; there are no card refunds. A purchase yields a
  durable grant bound to the exact content_hash purchased — a byte-identical
  body is always re-servable, a changed body is a new purchase.
- Creator payouts follow `revenue_share_policies` (currently `rs_2026_09_v1`).

## Accounts

You must control the wallet or agent key you authenticate with. We may suspend
accounts for acceptable-use violations; the ladder (throttle → shadowban →
suspend) is the public contract for moderation.
